Remember These 3 Easy Rules

Modal verbs are super easy because they never change!

1. No "S" for He/She/It

Never add -s or -ed.

✓ She can play
✗ She cans play
2. Never Say "to"

Use the base verb directly right after the modal.

✓ I must sleep
✗ I must to sleep
3. Easy Questions

Just put the modal verb in the front to ask a question!

✓ Can you help?
✗ Do you can help?
