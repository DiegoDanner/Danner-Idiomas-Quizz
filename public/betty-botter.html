Card 7 de 7 • Comprehension

📖 What does it mean?

The story is actually quite simple. Entenda para dar vida à entonação!

🛒

1. The Shopping

Betty Botter bought some butter, but when she tasted it, she said: “This butter's bitter.”

😖

2. Bitter Taste

Bitter means having a sharp, unpleasant taste that ruins food.

🥣

3. The Batter

Batter is the mixture used to make cakes, pancakes, or muffins.

🥞

4. Better Butter

So she bought better butter and made her bitter batter better!

✨ So, in simple English:

Betty bought bad-tasting butter. She didn't want to put it in her cake/pancake mixture, so she bought better butter instead.

And the whole joke is that butter, better, bitter, and batter sound extremely similar, making the sentence increasingly difficult to pronounce! 😄
